The Philosophy of Education

As a teacher, I believe everyone deserves the right to education. I believe one of my roles as an educator is to knock down as many barriers as possible to create accessible learning opportunities. All of my professional experience has led me to believe that all students are capable of learning as long as they are given the proper support.

I would like to use my skills in a multitude of settings. By utilizing my knowledge for public school education as well as for larger corporations, I can provide not only accessible learning, but also blended learning that supports all students. With my background in education, I have practical knowledge on how the programs we provide teachers are used, as I have used them in my own classes. I am inspired by Instructional Designers that have had experience using the technologies they expect educators to use and hope that with this program, I can do the same. This role impacts not only the people I currently oversee, but also the countless future lives that will cross through their rooms.

My Approach to the Future

I am in my first semester of my graduate student career. As I continue with my program, I will develop more competencies in tasks analysis to design learning experiences that pinpoint the needs of the environment. Pursuing graduate education is both a personal and professional milestone. I bring a strong work ethic that my immigrant parents taught me, a collaborative mindset that my coworkers helped coach, and a genuine desire to help individuals learn. This background has made me aware of issues of equity and inclusion in learning environments. Graduate study in Learning Technologies and Design is not only a step forward in my career, but also aligns with my personal values of providing meaningful education to other first-generation students and those who do not have access to support in education.

I believe that in life we are always learning. Learning isn’t limited to a classroom setting and my time spent with older adults proved that. To be a good educator, you have to learn alongside your students and colleagues. You are learning with them. Learning about your students, seeing what works best for them. A teacher is someone who can evolve and adapt their lessons.
